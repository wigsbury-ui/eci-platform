**Placement:** Left column above “Get in touch / Become an introduction partner” on `/agents`

**Tone:** Clear, practical, welcoming

## Spoken script (~95 words)

**[Open, to camera]**

If you want to introduce investors or operators to Ellesmere College International, this is where you start.

**[Two paths]**

You can join us as an introduction agent, covering markets and professional networks,  
or as a rainmaker, opening doors through trusted personal connections.

Both paths feed the same brand-licensing pipeline. We brief you, qualify the introduction, and lead the partnership.

**[How to apply, on this page]**

To get in touch, use the form on this page.

Enter your name, organisation, and email.  
Under Interest, choose Agent / Introduction Partner.  
In the message, tell us briefly whether you are applying as an agent or a rainmaker, which markets you know, and how you would like to help.

**[Close]**

Send the enquiry. We typically reply within three working days.

We look forward to hearing from you.

---

## Notes for production

- Film against a calm interior or campus backdrop; keep it direct to camera.
- Do not quote fee percentages or commission schedules on camera.
- End card can show: `international@ellesmere.com` and “Use the form on this page.”
