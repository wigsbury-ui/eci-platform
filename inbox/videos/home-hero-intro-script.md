# Home hero intro video: script

**Working title:** Ellesmere College: who we are  
**Length:** ~90 seconds  
**Tone:** Warm, authoritative, heritage-led; big-picture context, not a sales pitch  
**Audience:** Potential investors and introduction agents arriving on the homepage (also useful for anyone new to the brand)  
**Placement:** Homepage hero, right column (same pattern as the investors page hero video)  
**Suggested deliverable:** `public/videos/ellesmere-intro.mp4` (+ `public/videos/ellesmere-intro-poster.jpg`)  
**Do not duplicate:** Investors page `investor-intro.mp4` (growth / partnership) or `brand-licensing.mp4` (commercial offer). This film is **the school and its story**.

## Spoken script (~195 words)

**[Open: Shropshire campus, grounds, buildings, pupils in motion]**

For more than a century, Ellesmere College has stood on its Shropshire campus: one hundred and sixty acres of British boarding-school life, founded in eighteen eighty-four.

This is not a brand invented for export. It is a living school: classrooms, sport, boarding houses, and a community that has shaped generations of pupils.

**[Philosophy: learning, pastoral moments, choir or sport if available]**

Ellesmere is built on a simple ambition: **Life:Ready**.  
Education that prepares young people not only for examinations, but for character, confidence, and the world beyond school.

That sits inside High Performance Learning: rigorous British standards, strong pastoral care, and the belief that every child can excel when the culture around them expects it.

**[Bridge: international campuses, brief; heritage campus still visible in cutaways]**

Today, that same standard travels.  
From this historic campus to Ellesmere College Riyadh, open now in Al Hamra and Salwa, and Ellesmere College Doha, opening soon.

Same heritage. Same commitment. Families across the Middle East choosing a name they already associate with British excellence.

**[Close: presenter to camera, or slow campus pull-back]**

Ellesmere College International exists so that standard can grow responsibly, with partners who respect what the name means.

Whether you are exploring our schools, considering an investment, or introducing someone who should build with us: **this** is the foundation everything else rests on.

Ellesmere College. British excellence, globally delivered.

2. Campus life: classroom, sport, boarding, choir or assembly
